Places of Interest

Museum of Popular Cultures of Sonora

Museo de Culturas Populares e Indígenas de Sonora


Located in the historical downtown area, Museo de Culturas Populares e Indígenas de Sonora is a museum that offers exhibitions of the indigenous cultures of Sonora. The building dates back to the late 1800's

Address: Dr Hoeffer 22, El Centenario, 83260 Hermosillo, Son., Mexico

Museo Regional de Sonora

Museo Regional de Sonora


Established in 1985 and originally the cities Penitentiary constructed in 1908 the museum offers 18 exhibition halls, five temporary exhibits to promote the history and culture of Sonora.

Address: Jesús García Final s/n, C. California 35, La Matanza, 83080 Hermosillo, Son., Mexico

Musas Museo de arte de Sonora

Museo de Culturas Populares e Indígenas de Sonora


Located south of downtown, The Art Museum of Sonora was founded in 2009 and features collections of both Mexican and international artists. Open Tuesday to Sunday from 10:00am to 5:00pm.

Address: Centro Cultural Musas, Blvd. Agustín de Vildósola S/N, Proyecto Rio Sonora Hermosillo XXI, 83270 Hermosillo, Son., Mexico

Cerro de la Campana

La Campana


La Campana" is a notable hill in Hermosillo, One can drive up to the top using Cucurpe Street. Once at the top visitors can park and walk to a viewing platform to see the amazing Panoramic views. The stones are the hill are also know to produce a bell like sound.

Height: 319m or 1048 feet

Catedral de la Asunción

Metropolitan Cathedral of Asunción


Located in the Historical Downtown plaza and standing at 30 meters, construction of the Cathedral of Asunción begun in 1877 and was completed in 1908, however one of the domes was not finished until 1963. The Cathedrals; architecture features styles of Baroque , Neoclassical , Neo-Gothic. Nearby the Cathedral local vendors sell food and souvenirs

Address: Blvr. Miguel Hidalgo S/N, Centro Norte, 83000 Hermosillo, Son., Mexico
